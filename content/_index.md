I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence) at the
[University of Tehran](https://ut.ac.ir/en), working with
Dr. Bagher BabaAli on sequence models and representation learning. My current
work uses **time-series foundation models** for wearable and smartphone motion
sensors, from human activity recognition to clinical gait assessment and
detecting freezing of gait in Parkinson's disease. Our team won **1st place in
the Sussex-Huawei Locomotion (SHL) Challenge** in both 2025 and 2026. For my
thesis, I am building compact models for document-level offline **handwritten
text recognition** in English and Persian, using JEPA-style latent prediction.

Before that, I did my B.Sc. in Computer Science at Kharazmi University, where I
still work with Dr. Bardia Panahbehagh on spatial survey sampling, now also
using deep learning to learn and optimize sampling designs. We co-develop the
open-source [graphical-sampling](https://github.com/mehdimhb/graphical-sampling)
package.

Going forward, I want to work on machine learning for health, especially models
that keep learning from a patient's own wearable data after deployment. I will
finish my M.Sc. by June 2027 and am looking for PhD positions starting in 2027.
